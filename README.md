# 📦 Supply Chain Dashboard | Power BI

## 📖 Overview

This project presents an interactive **Supply Chain Dashboard** developed using **Microsoft Power BI**. The dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of key supply chain metrics, enabling users to monitor performance, identify trends, and support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Monitor overall supply chain performance.
- Analyze revenue and product sales across different product categories.
- Track inventory levels and product availability.
- Evaluate shipping performance and lead times.
- Compare sales across transportation modes, routes, and locations.
- Provide interactive filtering for deeper business insights.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- 💰 Total Revenue Generated
- 📦 Total Products Sold
- 📈 Total Stock Available
- 🚚 Average Shipping Cost
- ⏳ Average Lead Time

### Visualizations

- Products Sold by Transportation Mode
- Revenue Generated by Product Type
- Product Availability vs Products Sold
- Products Sold by Routes
- Products Sold & Revenue Generated by Location
- Shipping Time by Product Type

### Interactive Filters

- Product Type
- Shipping Carrier
- Inspection Results
- Routes
